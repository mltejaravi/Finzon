Eligibility criteria

  • Business vintageAt least 2 years in operation
  • TurnoverAnnual turnover of ₹20 Lakh or more
  • Age23 – 65 years for promoters
  • ProfitabilityProfit-making for the last 1 – 2 years
  • Credit scoreCIBIL score of 700+ preferred

Documents required
