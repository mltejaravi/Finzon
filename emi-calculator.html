The formula

How is EMI calculated?

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount you pay every month until your loan is repaid. It is calculated using:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]
  • P — PrincipalThe loan amount you borrow.
  • r — Monthly interest rateAnnual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100. For 9% p.a., r = 0.0075.
  • n — Number of instalmentsTenure in months. For 20 years, n = 240.
